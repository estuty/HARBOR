Move sites from cPanel

Bring over websites, databases, email accounts with all their mail, forwarders, DNS records and cron jobs from a cPanel backup. Passwords keep working.

1. Make a backup on the old host

  1. Sign in to cPanel on your old host and open Backup (or Backup Wizard).
  2. Click Download a Full Account Backup, choose Home Directory as the destination and generate it.
  3. When the email arrives, download the backup-….tar.gz file. On WHM, a cpmove-USER.tar.gz from Transfer Tool or pkgacct works too.

2. Give it to Harbor
